# My Favorite K-Drama: Business Proposal (사내맞선)

---

## Overview
**Business Proposal** is a hit South Korean romantic comedy television series starring *Ahn Hyo-seop*, *Kim Se-jeong*, *Kim Min-gue*, and *Seol In-ah*. It is based on the popular webtoon of the same title written by HaeHwa and illustrated by Narak.

> "I'm good at many things... including loving you."  
> — *Kang Tae-moo*

---

## Main Characters
* **Kang Tae-moo** (CEO of GO Food)
* **Shin Ha-ri** (Food Researcher / "Shin Geum-hui")
* **Cha Sung-hoon** (Tae-moo's Chief Secretary)
* **Jin Young-seo** (Ha-ri's Best Friend & Marine Group Heiress)

---

## Why I Love It
1. **Hilarious Misunderstandings:** Ha-ri going on a blind date in disguise to turn off Tae-moo backfires completely.
2. **Great Main & Second Couple Chemistry:** Both couples have amazing dynamics throughout the show.
3. **Fast-Paced & Energetic:** 12 episodes filled with comedy, romance, and zero boring moments.
